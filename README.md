# Content & Marketing Portfolio

Marketing portfolio for Ebrahim Arefi — brand strategy, copywriting, video
production, and e-commerce work, presented as a single-page site.

**Live:** https://ebi-ar.github.io/portfolio/

## What's in it

- **Brand strategy & storytelling** — positioning and brand-story decks for
  beauty, watch, and pet-care concepts
- **Copywriting & SEO** — product copy, campaign messaging, and keyword research
- **Video production** — long-form video essays and documentary-style pieces,
  scripted and edited end to end
- **E-commerce** — Shopify storefronts built and configured, from product pages
  to promotional campaigns

## Structure

- `index.html` — the whole site: About, Education, Skills, Work, Videos,
  Experience, and Contact
- `files/` — case studies and written samples as PDFs
- `videos/` — video work played inline on the page
- `images/` — thumbnails and campaign stills

## Notes

The site is static: no build step, no dependencies. It's served straight from
this repository by GitHub Pages, so pushing to `main` publishes it.
