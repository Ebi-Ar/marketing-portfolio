# portfolio

Content & Marketing Portfolio

Marketing portfolio for Ebrahim Arefi — brand strategy, copywriting, video production, and e-commerce work, presented as a single-page site.

What's in it — brand strategy & storytelling (positioning and brand-story decks for beauty, watch, and pet-care concepts) · copywriting & SEO (product copy, campaign messaging, keyword research) · video production (long-form video essays and documentary-style pieces, scripted and edited end to end) · e-commerce (Shopify storefronts built and configured)

Structure — index.html is the whole site (About, Education, Skills, Work, Videos, Experience, Contact); files/ holds PDF case studies; videos/ and images/ hold the media played inline.

Notes — static site, no build step, no dependencies. Pushing to main publishes it.
